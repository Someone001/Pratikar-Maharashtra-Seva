# Translation & Trauma-Informed Linguistic Review

**Languages Covered:** English (en), Hindi (hi - हिन्दी), Marathi (mr - मराठी)  
**Regulatory Context:** Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021; MeitY SOP on Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII, 11 Nov 2025).

---

## 1. Guiding Principles for Language Selection

Victims of sextortion, non-consensual image distribution, and synthetic deepfakes experience high levels of acute stress, shame, and fear. Linguistic choices within Pratikar adhere to three non-negotiable principles:

1. **Non-Victim-Blaming & Trauma-Informed Tone**: Language avoids loaded words like "leaked" or "compromised" that subtly imply carelessness by the affected individual. Instead, active legal phrasing focuses on the perpetrator's unlawful act: *"Non-consensual distribution"*, *"Threat to share"*, *"Morphed / fake depiction"*.
2. **Statutory Alignment with Indian Law**: Indian intermediary grievance officers and cybercrime police stations operate under statutory language defined by Rule 3(2)(b) of the IT Rules 2021. Using precise statutory terminology (e.g., *मध्यस्थ*, *तक्रार निवारण अधिकारी*, *संमती नसलेली अंतरंग सामग्री*) prevents intermediaries from rejecting notices as vague or non-statutory.
3. **Devanagari Cultural & Dialectical Clarity**: Hindi and Marathi phrasing favors clear, widely understood legal vocabulary rather than obscure Sanskritized neologisms or overly casual Western slang.

## 3. Rationale for Specific Key Phrasing

### 3.1 Why "संमती नसलेली अंतरंग सामग्री" Instead of Colloquial Words?
In Marathi, informal discourse frequently uses words borrowed from English like "लीक झालेले फोटो" (leaked photos) or problematic terms like "अश्लील फोटो" (obscene photos).
* Calling personal imagery "obscene" in Marathi implies moral wrongdoing or culpability on the victim's part.
* Calling it "leaked" implies an accident or negligence.
* In contrast, **"संमती नसलेली अंतरंग सामग्री"** firmly centers **consent** as the pivotal legal boundary. Under Rule 3(2)(b) and Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, the crime is the *distribution without consent*. This protects victim dignity while providing exact statutory wording for police FIRs and platform notices.

### 3.2 Age Gate & POCSO Messaging
When an affected person is under 18:
* **English:** *"Specialized child protection route required. If the person affected is under 18 years old, self-service takedown via this tool is not the right or safest route."*
* **Hindi:** *"18 वर्ष से कम आयु के व्यक्तियों के लिए विशेष संरक्षण आवश्यक है। यह टूल केवल वयस्कों के लिए है।"*
* **Marathi:** *"१८ वर्षांखालील व्यक्तींसाठी विशेष बाल संरक्षण कायदा लागू होतो. हे टूल फक्त १८ वर्षांवरील व्यक्तींसाठी आहे."*

The wording explicitly warns users **not** to save, forward, or possess child imagery, as possessing sexual imagery of minors is a severe offense under the POCSO Act. It directly surfaces **Childline 1098**, **112**, and the specialized POCSO wing of the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (`cybercrime.gov.in`).
